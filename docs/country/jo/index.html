🇯🇴 Jordan radio stations — stream URLs and M3U playlist

This playlist has 30 internet radio stations from Jordan, taken from the public-domain Radio Browser directory. That is every station from Jordan that passed the directory's last check, sorted by community votes. Each one passed Radio Browser's most recent stream check, but streams go offline or move, so a few may not play. The table gives every station's stream URL, for Wi-Fi radios and players that take one address at a time. Last updated 2026-10-26.
