Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator radio stations by country

Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator read their internet radio from one file, live_streams.sii. Below is a ready-made file for each of 150 countries, holding up to 100 of its most-voted stations that the games can play: MP3 and Ogg streams, from the public-domain Radio Browser directory, rebuilt every week.

How to install: Close the game, rename the live_streams.sii already in your Documents/Euro Truck Simulator 2 folder (or American Truck Simulator), put the downloaded file in its place under the same name, and start the game.

Streams belong to their stations and go offline or move from time to time, so a few entries may not play. 72FM is not connected with the games' makers.
